How to play

1Everyone gets an unusual word. Write a believable fake definition before time runs out.
2Read every definition and secretly vote for the one you think is real. No voting for your own!
3Earn 2 points for every friend fooled by your fake, or 1 point for finding the real meaning.
🏆  After 5 rounds, the player with the most points wins. Let the bluffing begin!
